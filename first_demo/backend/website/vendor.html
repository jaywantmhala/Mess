HUNGRILY FOODS VENDOR APP GUIDELINES

How The Vendor App & Mess Operations Work

Simple, transparent operating rules designed to help your Mess manage daily orders efficiently and maximize customer satisfaction.

CRITICAL RULE

Daily Menu Update before 9:00 AM

Update mandatory menu items (2 Suki Sabji & 2 Gravy Sabji for both Lunch & Dinner) every day before 9:00 AM. Only then will your Mess appear Online to Customers.

ORDER TIMINGS

Lunch & Dinner Order Slots

  • Lunch Orders: Accepted until 9:00 AM. Deliveries completed after 12:00 PM.
  • Dinner Orders: Accepted until 4:00 PM. Deliveries completed after 7:00 PM.
DELIVERY RADIUS

~2 KM Radius & Vendor Delivery

You will receive orders from customers located within a ~2 KM radius of your Mess. Delivery and empty Tiffin collection is the responsibility of the Vendor.

SECURE PROCESS

OTP-Based Delivery & Collection

Both Delivery and empty Tiffin Collection will be OTP-based, ensuring complete transparency, zero disputes, and secure order verification.

PAYOUT & TRACKING

Fixed Rates & Bank Payout (3-4 Days)

Per-Tiffin rate is fixed in advance for each city. Track your Orders and Delivered Tiffins directly in the App. Payout credited to your bank account within 3 to 4 days.

0% SERVICE FEE

Zero Service Fee & Free Mess Marketing

Hungrily Foods does not charge any Service Fee (only ~3% Payment Gateway Fee applicable). Plus, Hungrily Foods actively promotes and advertises your Mess to reach more customers!

VENDOR PROTOCOL & GUIDELINES

Strict Veg / Non-Veg Separation & Packaging Rules

To guarantee 100% customer trust and safety, on Non-Veg days (Wednesday, Friday, Sunday), all meat items must be prepared in dedicated cookware and sealed with Red Hungrily Tamper-Evident Tape. Veg dishes always use Green Tape, and sweets on Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday go in leak-proof dessert containers.
